# Routine project archive

For each significant change, assess what became obsolete: a document revision, rejected hypothesis, completed plan, replaced OpenSpec change or temporary workaround. Age alone is not a reason to archive. Identify the current source, reasons and incoming links. Preserve the decision meaning and a link to one primary evidence location beside the task or in the project archive. Update navigation and check links. Do not copy identical machine reports into two locations.

A rejected hypothesis does not change the current specification. Removing functionality needs a separate decision; archiving text does not authorize it. Integrate a native change with spec-integrate and its readiness check. Close a rejected change with spec-abandon and a reason, without changing current requirements. History remains in the store. For an explicitly retained OpenSpec route, use its standard command after checking the delta and result. On a path collision, unavailable evidence or unknown owner, keep the original and report the limit. Do not remove the only material evidence. Intermediate and duplicate results may be removed after checking ownership and links. Record a temporary workaround with its reason, owner and review condition. When normal operation returns, check that the workaround is no longer needed.
